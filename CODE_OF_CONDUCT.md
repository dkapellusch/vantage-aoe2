# Code of conduct

Vantage follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and respectful, assume good intent, give and take constructive feedback
gracefully, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, personal attacks and discriminatory
language are not tolerated in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainer privately through GitHub
([@dkapellusch](https://github.com/dkapellusch)). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments,
close threads, or ban participants who violate this code, following the Contributor Covenant's
enforcement guidelines.
